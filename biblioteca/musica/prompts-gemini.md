# Prompts de música para Gemini (director-akarti)

Se crean una vez y se reutilizan en todos los teasers. Cada pista: unos 60 s, instrumental, final definido. Guardarlas en `biblioteca/musica/` con el nombre indicado. Después medir con `python3 herramientas/akarti.py bpm ARCHIVO` y anotar en `indice.csv`.

## playa-luminosa (118 BPM)
Sunlit organic deep house for a luxury beach villa tour. 118 BPM, key of A major. Soft rounded four-on-the-floor kick, brushed shakers, warm Rhodes chords, a plucked nylon-string guitar motif, airy pads and a gentle wave-like noise swell. Structure: 0–4 s filtered intro with the guitar motif, the beat enters at 4 s, 4–44 s steady development adding Rhodes and pads, 44–56 s gentle lift, 56–60 s clean defined ending on the tonic chord. Total length 60 seconds. Instrumental only, clean professional mix.

## campo-calido (96 BPM)
Warm cinematic acoustic folk for a countryside estate tour. 96 BPM, key of D major. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft upright bass, light cajón, felt piano and a warm string swell. Structure: 0–4 s solo guitar intro, percussion enters at 4 s, 4–48 s development adding piano and strings, 48–58 s full warm lift, 58–60 s defined final chord. Total length 60 seconds. Instrumental only, clean professional mix.

## moderno-urbano (110 BPM)
Minimal modern electronic for a sleek city apartment tour. 110 BPM, key of F minor. Tight muted kick, crisp soft hi-hats, deep clean sub bass, a glassy synth arpeggio and soft felt-piano chords. Structure: 0–4 s arpeggio intro, beat enters at 4 s, 4–46 s development adding bass and piano, 46–56 s subtle build, 56–60 s precise ending on a sustained chord. Total length 60 seconds. Instrumental only, clean professional mix.

## lodge-andino (90 BPM)
Atmospheric organic downtempo for a mountain and nature lodge tour. 90 BPM, key of E minor. Soft frame drum, wooden percussion, a breathy bamboo-flute melody, warm analog pads and a gentle nylon guitar. Structure: 0–4 s pad and flute intro, percussion enters at 4 s, 4–46 s development adding guitar, 46–56 s open airy lift, 56–60 s calm defined ending. Total length 60 seconds. Instrumental only, clean professional mix.
